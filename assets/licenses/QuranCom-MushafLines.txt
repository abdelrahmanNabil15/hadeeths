Mushaf line layout (assets/quran/mushaf-madinah-lines.txt)

Which words of the Quran fall on which line of which page of the 15-line Madinah mushaf
(King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex, 1421H print), together with the Complex's own Uthmani
Hafs text, as published by the Quran.com API v4 (https://api.quran.com, fields text_qpc_hafs,
v2_page and line_v2). Retrieved 2026-11-21 and rearranged into one line per mushaf line by
tool/build_mushaf_lines.dart; no word was changed.

The printed layout and the Uthmani Hafs text are the work of the King Fahd Glorious Quran
Printing Complex, Al-Madinah Al-Munawwarah.
